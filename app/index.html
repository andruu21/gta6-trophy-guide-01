Completion Roadmap projected

Phase 1 — Story blind run (≈ 0–70 h)

Play the story once with spoiler mode ON. Keep cheats OFF the entire game (they disable trophies). Do story missions as they come, and grab every trophy that pops naturally: chapter clears, 'Trust', 'Break the Bank', the partnership awards, 'In The Beginning…' on Ocean Beach in hour one, and 'Capital Offense' whenever a showroom tempts you.

Phase 2 — Pre-endgame sweep (before the final mission)

Before triggering 'You Gotta Know When to Get Off', clear anything chapter-locked: all stranger strands ('Strangers & Freaks' and the five named finales), the story heists, and every district you can reach ('The Goodtime State'). Start feeding the relationship meter and the laundering loop now — they need accumulated time, not a late grind.

Phase 3 — Post-story world clean-up (≈ 70–120 h)

After the credits, the map is fully open. Work state-wide: 'Whip It Good' (100 vehicles), 'On the Spot' (10 registrations), 'Challenging Tank' and 'Catch Me If You Can' now that military bases and VCIA are open, the robbery trophies ('Sneakthief', 'Maximum Withdrawal', 'A Classic Blitz Play', 'Pest Control'), and the money trophies ('Dime on the Dollar', 'Treat Yo Self', 'Little Boxes').

Phase 4 — The grinds (≈ 120–180 h)

The time-heavy trio: 250 stunts ('Cunning Stunts'), 100 WAiNK locations ('You WAiNKer!'), 50 Challenges ('One Last Thing'), plus meditation spots, three collectible card sets, and 'Master of None' if any activity type is still untouched. Chain these: one session per district covers stunts, WAiNKs and meditation together.

Phase 5 — Skill checks & 100% (final stretch)

Save the skill trophies for the endgame arsenal: gold in all Shooting Range challenges ('The Shootist'), 20 race wins, every Top Fun course, 'Gunslinger' and 'Hot Together'. Then finish the 100% checklist for 'A Long Road' — by now it's mostly bookkeeping. The platinum, 'Vice Got a Price!', unlocks with the checklist's last item.

Missables & What To Watch projected

Nothing in the projected list is permanently missable post-credits — but these are the traps that cost restarts or 20-hour replays if you ignore them.

  • Cheats kill trophies. Cheat codes reportedly disable trophies and saving. Start a cheat-free discipline on day one; you cannot undo a flagged save.
  • Chapter-locked content. VCIA ('Catch Me If You Can'), military installations ('Challenging Tank'), and several districts only open as the story progresses. Don't expect a fully explorable state in Chapter 1.
  • Final-mission lockout risk. Trigger the endgame only after clearing stranger strands and story heists. Projected sources say free-roam persists after credits, but checkpoint-locked story windows do not reopen.
  • Heist approach choices. 'The Cartel Heist' planning board may lock one-time loot variants per approach. Save before planning if you want to see both routes.
  • Stealth attempts reset hard. 'Sneakthief' fails the moment one guard is alerted. Save before entering the store; treat an alerted NPC as a restart prompt.
  • Relationship is cumulative. 'Personal Best' cannot be ground in one sitting — date nights, meals and hand-holding need to accrue across the whole run. Feed it from Chapter 2 onward.
  • Bank-vault escapes must complete. 'Maximum Withdrawal' only counts vaults you escape cleanly — dying after the grab voids the attempt.
  • One online caveat. The launch list is single-player only; GTA Online is not confirmed to carry trophies if and when it arrives. Finish the list in story mode, not on a future-online promise.
